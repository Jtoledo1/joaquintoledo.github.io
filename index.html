CV

I am a PhD student in Economics at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. My research focuses on urban economics, particularly land-use regulation, housing markets, and urban development, using microeconometric and spatial methods.

I have worked as a consultant on public policy and teach quantitative methods and economics.

Research

Property Taxes as Market Signals
Hugo E. Silva, Joaquín Toledo Tesoro
SSRN

Property tax liability increases housing prices by ~4%, acting as a certification mechanism that reduces information asymmetry in opaque markets.

Property Taxes and Rental Prices
Matias Brum, Joaquín Toledo Tesoro
SSRN

A cadastral reassessment raises rental prices by 9.3%, implying inelastic supply and highlighting institutional frictions in tax incidence.

When Do Land-Use Regulations Matter?
Joaquín Toledo Tesoro, Diego Gil, Hugo Silva
PDF (coming soon)

FAR constraints bind strongly, while height restrictions matter only under enforcement; otherwise they create uncertainty and are discounted in prices.

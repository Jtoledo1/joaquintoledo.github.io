About

I am a PhD student in Economics at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. My research lies in urban economics, with a particular focus on land-use regulation, housing markets, and urban development. Methodologically, I rely heavily on microeconometric techniques and spatial data to study how regulatory constraints shape prices, supply, and city structure.

Beyond academia, I have worked as a consultant on public policy, collaborating with public sector institutions on issues related to urban regulation, housing, and local development. I also have teaching experience in quantitative methods and economics.

I am originally from Uruguay. Outside of research, I play basketball at an amateur level, enjoy solving puzzles, and am a long-time supporter of Peñarol.

About

I am a PhD student in Economics at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. My research focuses on urban economics, with particular interest in land-use regulation, housing supply, and urban development. I use administrative data, spatial methods, and quasi-experimental designs to study how regulation shapes prices and city structure.

Research

When Do Land-Use Regulations Matter? Stringency, Compliance, and Urban Development in Santiago
with coauthors

This paper studies how the effects of land-use regulation depend on both regulatory stringency and local compliance, using detailed parcel-level data and spatial variation across municipalities in Santiago.

SSRN
Housing Supply and Regulatory Constraints

This paper examines how zoning and regulatory constraints affect housing supply decisions and development patterns using building permit data and quasi-experimental variation.
